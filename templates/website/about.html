ABOUT US

StyloTech Team is a forward-thinking technology company dedicated to protecting organizations from evolving cyber threats while helping businesses grow through innovative IT solutions. Founded in 2021, StyloTech was previously known as JPCS Consultancy and has rapidly grown into a trusted name in Technology Services, IT Consulting, Cybersecurity, and Digital Forensics. At StyloTech, we understand that the modern digital world brings both opportunities and risks. As organizations adopt advanced technologies, cloud infrastructure, and interconnected systems, the threat landscape continues to expand. Our mission is to help businesses stay secure, resilient, and prepared for the challenges of tomorrow.

Our Expertise

01
Cybersecurity
Solutions

We provide advanced cybersecurity solutions to protect organizations from cyber threats, data breaches, and system vulnerabilities. Our experts conduct vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, threat monitoring, and security audits to keep your systems secure.

Web & Mobile Application Development

We design and develop secure, scalable web and mobile applications tailored to business needs. Our development process focuses on performance, security, and user experience.

Secure Code Review / SSDLC Training

We help development teams build secure software by reviewing application code for vulnerabilities and implementing Secure Software Development Life Cycle (SSDLC) practices. Our training programs ensure developers follow industry-standard security practices.
