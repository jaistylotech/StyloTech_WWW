Drone Battery Management System (BMS)

{% endcomment %}

A Drone Battery Management System (BMS) is a device that helps monitor and protect the battery used in drones. It keeps track of important things like voltage, current, and temperature to make sure the battery is working safely and efficiently.


The system can detect problems early, such as overheating or faults, and helps prevent damage to the battery and the drone. It is designed for high-capacity lithium batteries and supports battery packs from 6S to 14S.


This BMS is built to work reliably even in harsh or extreme environments, making it suitable for UAVs and drones used in demanding conditions

Defence Battery Management System (BMS)

{% endcomment %}

The Defence BMS System is a high-reliability battery management solution designed for 3S lithium battery packs operating between 7.5V and 12.6V in harsh and mission-critical environments.


It provides protection against over-voltage, under-voltage, overcurrent, short circuits, and thermal faults while ensuring safe operation.


Supports UART/isoUART communication for real-time monitoring and seamless system integration with rugged EMI/EMC protection.
