CERTIFICATIONS

CERTIFIED ETHICAL HACKER

Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) is a professional cybersecurity certification that teaches how to identify, test, and secure computer systems from hackers using the same tools and techniques that attackers use.

COMPUTER HACKING FORENSIC INVESTIGATOR (CHFI)

Computer Hacking Forensic Investigator (CHFI) trains professionals to detect cyberattacks, investigate incidents, and collect digital evidence from systems and networks to determine attack methods.

OFFENSIVE SECURITY CERTIFIED PROFESSIONAL (OSCP)

Offensive Security Certified Professional (OSCP) teaches identifying vulnerabilities, exploiting systems, and gaining access ethically, simulating real hacker techniques for penetration testing skills.

COMPTIA SECURITY+

CompTIA Security+ is a global entry-level cybersecurity certification that teaches essential skills to protect and defend computer systems, networks, and data from various cyber threats effectively.

GIAC SECURE SOFTWARE PROGRAMMER (GSSP)

GIAC Secure Software Programmer (GSSP) certification focuses on secure coding practices, helping developers build applications resistant to vulnerabilities and common cyber threats effectively and securely.

SHRM CERTIFIED PROFESSIONAL (SHRM-CP)

SHRM Certified Professional (SHRM‑CP) is an internationally recognized Human Resource (HR) certification for professionals who work in HR operations, employee management, and people management.
